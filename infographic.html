AI in Healthcare:
Helpful Tool or Too Much Trust?

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of everyday healthcare. It can help physicians save time and process information, but its growing role raises an important question: how much should we rely on it when people's health is involved?

AI is entering healthcare fast.

The percentage of physicians reporting that they incorporate at least one AI use case into their practice has increased considerably in only a few years.
