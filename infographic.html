AI in Healthcare:
Helpful Tool or Too Much Trust?

        

            Artificial intelligence is becoming part of everyday healthcare.             It can help physicians save time and process information, but its             growing role raises an important question: how much should we rely             on it when people's health is involved?         

    
    
        
            

AI ADOPTION

            

                AI is entering healthcare fast.             

            

                The percentage of physicians reporting that they incorporate                 at least one AI use case into their practice has increased                 considerably in only a few years.             

            
                
                    2023                     38%                     
                        
                    
                
                
                    2024                     48%                     
                        
                    
                
                
                    2026                     72%                     
                        
                    
                
            
            

                Physicians incorporating at least one AI use case into their practice

THE PATIENT PERSPECTIVE

            

                But are patients ready to trust it?             

            

                As AI becomes more common in healthcare, many Americans are                 still cautious about how much it should be involved in                 decisions about their health.             

            
                
                    60%                     

                        of Americans said they would feel uncomfortable if                         their healthcare provider relied on AI for diagnosis                         or treatment recommendations.                     

                
                
                    75%                     

                        said they were more concerned that healthcare providers                         would adopt AI too quickly before fully understanding                         its risks.
